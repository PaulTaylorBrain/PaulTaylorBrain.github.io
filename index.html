How does the brain's processing of the visual world depend on what we are doing? We study how attention, prediction and action shape perception, particularly as we move through complex and changing environments.

Our research combines psychophysics with EEG, machine learning and non-invasive brain stimulation. Current projects investigate how we see the world as we move through it, and develop multivariate pattern analysis for EEG.
